How it runs

Ten days, clearly planned.

Before arrival

Dates, brief & content plan

You choose the dates that fit your occupancy — we book flights around them. A short call about goals, offers and brand rules, then a shot list for your approval.

Days 1–3

Hero shoot

Rooms, pools, beach, restaurant and drone. The first Reels go live while we're still there.

Days 4–8

Family series & AI studio

Kids Club, Camp Out, junior chef, wellness. In parallel we set up the AI system and run workshops for your team.

Days 9–10

Final shots & handover

Last scenes, raw library handed over, publishing calendar agreed.

After check-out

Editing, publishing, report

Final edits delivered within 2–3 weeks, YouTube episodes released, online lessons for staff, and a report with reach and engagement.
